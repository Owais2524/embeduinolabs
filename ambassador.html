PROGRAM OVERVIEW

What Is the Ambassador Program?

A community initiative connecting passionate engineering students across Pakistan to Embeduino Labs.

Pakistan has thousands of engineering students every year. Most graduate without ever touching a real microcontroller, designing a real PCB, or building a real embedded system. That's the problem we're solving.

The Campus Ambassador Program is how we scale that mission. You become the bridge between Embeduino Labs and your campus spreading awareness, building community, and helping your peers find a path into practical engineering.

You don't need to be an expert. You need to care about building a real engineering culture at your university.

🚀
DRIVEN STUDENTS FROM EVERY CAMPUS

We're building a network of motivated students from universities across Pakistan. Represent Embeduino Labs at your campus, spread awareness, grow the community, and shape the future of engineering education here.

ELIGIBILITY

Who Can Apply?

Simple requirements. Open to almost everyone from Pakistan's engineering community.

YOU SHOULD BE

  • A student at any Pakistani university
  • Genuinely passionate about engineering and technology
  • Willing to spread awareness at your campus
  • Active on at least one social platform (WhatsApp/Instagram/LinkedIn)
  • A self-starter who doesn't need to be micromanaged
  • Interested in embedded systems, PCB design, or IoT (even as a beginner)

YOU DON'T NEED TO BE

  • An expert in embedded systems or electronics
  • A final-year student. All years are welcome
  • From a specific discipline, EE, CS, Mechatronics, and beyond
  • Already enrolled in any Embeduino Labs course
  • A high GPA student. We care about drive, not grades
  • Based in any specific city. All cities across Pakistan
PROCESS

How It Works

Four simple steps from application to ambassador.

STEP 01 📋

Apply Online

Fill out the short application form. Tell us about yourself, your university, and why you want to represent Embeduino Labs.

STEP 02 🔍

Profile Screening

We review your application and shortlist candidates based on your drive, attitude, and community spirit, not your grades.

STEP 03 ⚡

Complete Tasks

Shortlisted candidates receive a small set of tasks. Complete them, show us how you show up when it counts.

STEP 04 🏆

Get Recognised

Candidates who complete the tasks are recognised as official ambassadors and receive all program perks and certification.

Common questions

Everything to know before you register.

Getting started

Do I need any experience?

Embedded Systems and PCB Design Level 1 are for complete beginners. STM32 Level 1 needs Arduino and embedded C basics, and PCB Design Level 2 needs Level 1 or equivalent experience.

Which course should I take first?

Embedded first and PCB second is the natural order, but both Level 1 courses suit beginners and can be taken together if you have the time.

Who is this for?

University students, working professionals and hobbyists who want to build real hardware skills.

Format

Which language are the sessions in?

A mix of Urdu and English. Explanations are in Urdu, while technical terms and code stay in English.

Are sessions live or recorded?

Sessions are recorded and available to everyone enrolled, so you never fall behind.

How long is each course?

Every course runs for 4 weeks, fully online.

Hardware and software

What hardware does the Embedded course need?

An Arduino board (Uno or Nano), an ESP8266 module (NodeMCU) and a basic component kit with breadboard, resistors, LEDs, sensors, servo and DC motors. The full list is shared before the course starts.

What do I need for PCB Design?

A laptop with KiCad and EasyEDA installed, both free. You can optionally order your board from JLCPCB at the end.

Yes. Each certificate has a credential ID that anyone can check on the verification page.

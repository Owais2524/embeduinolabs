Common Questions

Everything you need to know before registering. Courses, Pricing, Format and What to expect.

GETTING STARTED
No. Absolutely none. Both the Embedded Systems & IoT Bootcamp and the PCB Design Bootcamp are built for complete beginners. The Embedded & IoT Bootcamp starts from Arduino & C programming fundamentals. The PCB Design Bootcamp starts from zero, no prior electronics or PCB experience needed.
Everyone. The bootcamp is designed for anyone starting from zero. University Students, Working Professionals expanding into embedded systems, Career Switchers, and Hobbyists who want to go beyond YouTube tutorials. If you want to build real hardware skills, you're in the right place.
Both courses are beginner-friendly, and you can start with either based on your interest. Both Phase 1 courses run simultaneously though, so you can take both at the same time for a more complete embedded systems experience, if you have the capacity.
PRICING & REGISTRATION
Both courses are priced at PKR 5,000 each. Early bird registrants get 30% off, bringing it to PKR 3,500 per course. Register via the Google Form and complete payment to lock your seat. First come, first served.
Yes 30% off, guaranteed. Register via the Google Form before the early bird deadline and complete payment to lock in your discounted rate. Limited spots, when they're gone, they're gone.
Payment details are shared after you submit the registration form. We support common Pakistani payment methods. You'll receive full instructions via WhatsApp or email once your form is submitted.
FORMAT & DELIVERY
The course is delivered in a mix of Urdu and English. Teaching and explanations in Urdu, technical terms and code in English. This keeps it fully accessible without compromising the technical depth you need to actually work in the field.
Sessions will be recorded and available to all enrolled students. Live session details are finalised based on registrant preferences, you'll be asked about your preferred schedule and time slots will be finalized on community vote. Your input shapes how we deliver. You'll never fall behind.
Both Phase 1 courses are 4 weeks long, running on weekends (Saturday, Sunday), fully online. They start 13 June 2026.
HARDWARE & EQUIPMENT
An Arduino board (Uno or Nano), an ESP8266 module (NodeMCU), and a basic component kit (breadboard, resistors, LEDs, sensors, servo & dc motors). The full hardware list will be shared with registered students before launch. Total cost is typically under PKR 3,000 locally.
A laptop with KiCad and/or EasyEDA installed (both free). No special hardware needed, you'll design your board digitally and optionally order it from JLCPCB at the end. Full software setup guide is shared before Day 1.
CERTIFICATION & NEXT STEPS
Yes. Students who complete the course receive a certificate of completion from Embeduino Labs. We're also working on project-based assessments so your certificate reflects real, demonstrable skills not just attendance.
Phase 2 (starting July) introduces STM32 Embedded Development L1 and PCB Design Bootcamp L2 both open for waitlist now. Further courses planned include ESP32 & Advanced IoT, STM32 L2, nRF52 & BLE, and an advanced track covering Embedded CV, Edge AI, and TinyML.

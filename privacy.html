Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and who sees it.

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Embeduino Labs is an embedded systems engineering and training business based in Pakistan. Contact: embeduinolabs@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

  • Project brief form: your name, email, organisation, client type, services, budget, timeline and description. We use it to reply to your enquiry. It reaches us by email.
  • Course registration, waitlist and ambassador forms: these are Google Forms. Google processes what you enter under its own privacy policy.
  • Certificate verification: when you enter a credential ID, the page shows the student name, course, batch, completion date, grade and instructor for that certificate. Anyone who has a credential ID can see these details.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads.

Who we share data with

We use trusted service providers to receive form submissions and to store certificate records. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept as long as needed to deal with them. Certificate records are kept so certificates can be verified.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete data we hold about you, email embeduinolabs@gmail.com.

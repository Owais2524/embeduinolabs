What we build

Pick one service or hand us the whole project. We cover the path from idea to working hardware.

Embedded systems

Firmware and system design for microcontrollers, in bare metal C or on an RTOS. Built to be reliable first and fast second.

STM32 / ESP32 / Arduino / nRF52

IoT and connectivity

Sensors, wireless links, cloud pipelines and dashboards, wired together so the data actually arrives.

WiFi / BLE / LoRa / MQTT

PCB design

From schematic to manufacturing-ready Gerbers. Single layer through multi-layer boards, designed for signal integrity and for the fab that will build them.

KiCad / EasyEDA / multi-layer / DFM

Firmware development

Drivers, communication protocols, bootloaders and over-the-air updates for hardware you already have.

FreeRTOS / UART, I2C, SPI / DMA / OTA

Prototype to production

Take an idea from sketch to a working prototype, then to a design that is ready to manufacture.

Concept / prototype / production

3D design and assembly

Enclosures for your electronics, 3D printed prototypes and assembly guidance for small production runs.

Enclosures / 3D printing / assembly

Consultation and debugging

An outside engineer to weigh hardware choices, review an architecture or find out why a board misbehaves.

Design review / debugging

Testing and validation

Hardware and firmware testing against your specification before anything goes to production.

Test plans / validation

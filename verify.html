CREDENTIAL VERIFICATION

Verify a Certificate

Enter a credential ID from an Embeduino Labs certificate to confirm its authenticity and view the student's details.

Format: EL-[COURSE]-[YR][BATCH]-[ID]  ·  Example: EL-ESIOT-26B2-017

How Verification Works

Every Embeduino Labs certificate carries a unique credential ID. This page lets anyone confirm a certificate is genuine.

01

Find your credential ID

Your certificate has a unique credential ID printed at the bottom — formatted as EL-[COURSE]-[YR][BATCH]-[ID]. It's also sent to you via email when your certificate is issued.

02

Enter it above

Type or paste the full credential ID into the field above and click Verify. The system checks it against our certificate registry.

03

View or print the certificate

If the credential is valid, the certificate details are displayed — student name, course, batch, date of completion, and grade. You can also print a clean copy directly from this page.

04

Share the link

You can share a direct verification URL with employers or institutions: embeduinolabs.com/verify?id=EL-ESIOT-26B1-001

Credential Format

Each credential encodes the course, year, batch, and student number.
